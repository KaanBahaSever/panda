# Skip by vote: with three people in the channel, two votes skip a song someone else asked for.
s voteSkip=true announce=false
P test tone
+ Listener One
+ Listener Two
c !skip
e Vote to skip: 1/2.
c !voteskip
e Vote to skip: 1/2.
o !skip
e Skipped by vote (2/2).
# Cutting someone else's song short in other ways takes the vote too.
P test tone
c !playnow test tone
e vote with [b]!voteskip[/b]
c !stop
e vote with [b]!voteskip[/b]
c !np
e Now playing
c !skip
o !skip
e Skipped by vote
# Whoever asked for the song skips it at once.
c !play test tone
c !skip
e Skipped.
# With voting off and other people's songs open to everyone, !skip skips at once.
s voteSkip=false othersSongs=everyone
P test tone
c !skip
e Skipped.
